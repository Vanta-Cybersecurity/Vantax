How it works.

  1. Every sale counts

    Subscriptions, migration packages, chain licences and VantaxBot licences: 5% of each is due for burning.

  2. Bought on the open market

    Once a day, what's due buys $VANTAX.

  3. Burned for good

    The tokens go through the token program's burn, which removes them from the supply. Not a transfer to a "dead" wallet.

  4. Checked before it's shown

    The API reads both transactions on Solana before a burn appears here.
