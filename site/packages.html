Paying.

Which currencies can I pay in?

By card, any currency Stripe supports, and you see the amount in your currency before you pay. On Solana, SOL, USDC, USDT and $VANTAX are taken directly, and almost any other token is swapped to USDC by Jupiter in the same transaction.

Why are some prices in SOL and others in pounds or dollars?

Products sold on Solana are priced in SOL. Subscriptions are billed by Stripe in pounds. Migration work is quoted in US dollars, as most of it is for teams outside the UK. Whichever way it's priced, you can pay in any of the ways above, and the converted amount shown here is a guide at today's rate.

Do I need $VANTAX to buy anything?

No. Every product works without the token. Holders get the Holder API plan and a lower VantaxBot price.

Where does the money go?

Into building Vantax, which is one developer with no outside funding. 5% of every sale goes to buying back and burning $VANTAX, and each burn is published with its transaction.

Is anything held on my behalf?

No. Vantax never holds customer funds or keys. VantaxBot and Vantax Chains run on your own machines.
