Stay safe

This page only asks your wallet to sign a text message. If your wallet shows a transaction, an approval or a request to send anything, reject it: that isn't us.

Vantax will never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and never asks you to send tokens.

How it works

Your key is on the Holder plan while the wallet holds the minimum. Vantax checks the balance again about every 6 hours; below the minimum the key moves to the Free plan, and it comes back once the wallet holds enough again. One key per wallet.

The same signature check gives the holder price on VantaxBot, at checkout.
