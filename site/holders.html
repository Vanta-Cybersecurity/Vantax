Get your key

Your API key (holder)

This is the only time the key is shown. Store it in a password manager or secret store now. We keep only a hash of it. Lost it? Come back and sign again: you get a new key and the old one stops.

Stay safe. This page only asks your wallet to sign a text message. If your wallet shows a transaction, an approval or a request to send anything, reject it: that isn't us. Vantax will never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and never asks you to send tokens.

How it works: your key is on the Holder plan while the wallet holds the minimum. Vantax checks the balance again about every 6 hours; below the minimum the key moves to the Free plan, and it comes back once the wallet holds enough again. One key per wallet. You don't need the token to use Vantax: anyone can subscribe to Pro or Enterprise. Holder access can change or end. This is not financial advice or an invitation to buy; cryptoassets are high risk.
