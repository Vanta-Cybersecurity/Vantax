Recompute any outcome.

The same integer-only algorithm the operator runs: a ChaCha20 stream keyed by the server seed, your client seed and the nonce, with rejection sampling so no result is more likely than another.

Post-quantum signatures, in your browser.

The same ML-DSA-87 (NIST FIPS 204) keys and signatures as the vantax command line, compiled to WebAssembly. A key made here verifies with the CLI and back. For the compact scheme, see Falcon-512.

Signed receipts on every bet

ML-DSA-87 signatures bind you to each outcome before the seed is revealed. Players verify with free tools, including this page.

Seeds you can't lose or reuse

Per-player committed seeds derived from one master secret, sealed with AES-256-GCM. Rotation publishes a signed reveal.

An audit log that restarts you exactly

Every state change is hash-chained and stored before the engine answers; replaying the log rebuilds its state after a crash.

Round games and RNG reports

Seed chains commit to millions of crash rounds with one hash. Signed, reproducible chi-square and RTP reports for every game.

Open, specified and checkable.

The fairness algorithm is specified in full, with test vectors, and four implementations check each other: Rust, this WebAssembly build, the CLI and a stand-alone Python script. None of it has had an independent third-party audit yet. A passing check proves the outcome followed the published rules; it says nothing about an operator's licence or solvency.
