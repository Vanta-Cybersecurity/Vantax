Pre-release. Not yet audited.

The fairness algorithm is specified in full, with test vectors, and four implementations check each other (Rust, this WebAssembly build, the CLI and a stand-alone Python script). None of it has had an independent third-party audit yet. A passing check proves the outcome followed the published rules; it says nothing about an operator's licence or solvency.
