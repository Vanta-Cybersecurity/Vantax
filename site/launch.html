What you get

          
    
            
  • The node software. One program for Linux (x86-64) that runs a validator, syncs with your other nodes, serves a JSON API and hosts a block explorer for your chain.
    • 
            
  • Post-quantum signatures. Every account, transaction and block is signed with ML-DSA-87 (NIST FIPS 204), not the elliptic-curve signatures a quantum computer would break.
    • 
            
  • A signed licence. Your chain's name, ticker and supply rules, and your Solana wallet, signed with the Vantax issuer key. It's built into your chain's first block.
    • 
            
  • Validators you choose. 1 to 21 validators take turns making blocks (proof of authority). The chain keeps going while more than half of them are online.
    • 
            
  • Balances checked in every block. Nodes refuse any block that spends more than an account holds, reuses a nonce or mints more than the reward.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Stay safe

          
The only transaction on this page is the one-off payment of 6.5 SOL to the Vantax launch wallet, and your wallet shows it before you approve. Vantax will never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and never DMs you a payment address. Questions: @VantaxFounder.

        

        
You're buying a software licence, not an investment: Vantax doesn't host, run or promote your chain, and has no share in it. If you offer your chain's coins to other people, the rules where you and they live apply to you. Vantax Chains is new software and hasn't had an independent security audit yet; test your network before you rely on it. The node software runs on Linux x86-64; other systems will follow. Licences are signed with ML-DSA-87; the issuer key is shown by the Vantax API. Check it's live yourself: the launch API answers with the price and the downloads on offer.
