A Solana trading bot that checks before it buys.

VantaxBot watches the X accounts and Telegram channels you trust. When several of them call the same token, it checks the market, then runs the Vantax anti-rug scanner on the contract, the holders, the trading and the creator. Only a token that passes gets bought.

How it decides.

Six checks, in this order. A token has to clear every one before a single lamport moves.

  1. Hears a call

    It reads every post and message from your sources, pulls out contract addresses and DexScreener, Photon or Axiom links, and confirms on-chain that each one is a real token.

  2. Waits for agreement

    Each source has a weight you choose. Calls add up and fade with a 10-minute half-life, and nothing triggers until at least two different sources agree and the score passes your threshold.

  3. Checks the market

    Liquidity, market cap, five-minute volume, how much of it is buying, and how old the pair is. A token that has already run too far is skipped.

  4. Runs the anti-rug scan

    Authorities, LP lock or burn, Token-2022 traps, holder concentration and launch bundling, wash trading, a sell round-trip test, and the creator's history. Any critical flag blocks the buy.

  5. Sizes and buys

    Within your per-trade size, open-position and total-exposure limits, through Jupiter or straight to the pump.fun curve, landed with a Jito bundle.

  6. Guards the position

    A take-profit ladder, a stop-loss and a trailing stop, plus an exit the moment liquidity is pulled, the developer dumps or the pool drains.

You set the rules.

One config file, read at start-up. Everything below has a safe default, so a fresh install trades nothing until you say so.

  • Telegram remote/status, /positions, /sell, /scan, /kill and /resume from your phone, answered only in the chats you allow.
  • DashboardLive positions, every signal and why it was taken or skipped, scan reports and P&L, on your own machine.
  • Hard limitsA daily loss limit, a cap on trades per minute and a kill switch that stops new buys at once.
# vantaxbot.toml (part)
[bot]
mode = "paper"            # "live" needs a licence

[signals]
min_sources = 2           # independent accounts or chats
min_conviction = 70       # 0-100
instant_weight = 8        # one source this trusted is enough
window = "30m"
half_life = "10m"

[[signals.x.accounts]]
name = "a_caller_you_trust"
weight = 7                # 1 (noise) to 10 (your best)

[[signals.telegram.chats]]
id = -1001234567890
name = "your alpha group"
weight = 5

[scanner]
min_safe_score = 70       # critical flags always block

[trading]
buy_sol_max = 0.25
max_open_positions = 4
max_exposure_sol = 1.0

[trading.exits]
stop_loss_pct = 30
trailing_activate_pct = 40
trailing_stop_pct = 20

[risk]
max_daily_loss_sol = 1.0

Questions.

Does Vantax ever hold my funds or keys?

No. The bot runs on your machine, signs with your wallet's key on your machine, and talks to Solana directly. Vantax never sees your key, your balance or your trades.

Will it make me money?

Nobody can promise that, and we don't. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. The scanner lowers the chance of buying an outright rug; it can't remove market risk. Start in paper mode, and only trade what you can afford to lose.

Which currencies can I pay in?

Any card currency Stripe supports, through Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay or Google Pay. On Solana: SOL, USDC, USDT, $VANTAX, or any token Jupiter can route, swapped to USDC in the same transaction.

Can I move my licence to another wallet?

Yes, once in a while. Message @VantaxFounder from the wallet's owner and we'll reissue it for the new address.

What happens when a Monthly licence ends?

The bot finishes managing open positions and stops opening new ones. Paper mode keeps working. Buying again adds 30 days to the end of your current licence.

Refunds?

Try it in paper mode first, it's free. Because the licence and download are delivered straight away, you agree at checkout to lose the 14-day cancellation right. If something is broken on our side, message @VantaxFounder and we'll put it right.
